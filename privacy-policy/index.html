When you request an estimate or contact Even Solutions, we may collect your name, email address, telephone number, property address, project details, communication preferences, and technical information associated with the submission, such as its date, time, and IP address.

We use this information to respond to inquiries, prepare and discuss estimates, schedule services, provide project-related customer care, maintain records, prevent fraud, and operate and improve our website and services.

Text messaging information

If you separately opt in to text messages (an optional, unchecked box on our estimate form, or by texting us first), Even Solutions may use your mobile number to send messages about your estimate, scheduling, and project updates. We do not send marketing or promotional text messages. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Consent to text messaging is not a condition of purchasing goods or services.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be sold, rented, or shared with any third parties, except service providers that help us deliver the messages (such as our messaging provider).

Service providers

We may provide information to vendors that process it on our behalf, such as website hosting, email, customer relationship management, and telecommunications providers. These providers may use the information only to perform services for Even Solutions and are not authorized to use mobile opt-in information for their own marketing.

Your choices

You may decline SMS consent and still submit a quote request. You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. To request access, correction, or deletion of your information, contact us using the information below.
